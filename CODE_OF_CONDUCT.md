# Code of conduct

Durastep follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else it is
represented, is expected to make it a harassment-free experience for everyone.

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome different viewpoints and experience, give and accept constructive
feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks,
and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub, using the contact options on the
maintainer's profile. For anything sensitive that must not be public, you can also use the repository's private
reporting on the **Security** tab. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person
who reports is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may ban, temporarily or
permanently, anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
